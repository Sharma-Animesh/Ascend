# Character art slots

Drop full-body character images here and the app uses them automatically in the
archetype carousel, reveal screen and anywhere else `CharacterArt` renders,
replacing the built-in vector figures.

| File | Archetype |
|---|---|
| `phantom.png` | Phantom — very lean, defined |
| `blade.png` | Blade — lean athletic |
| `vanguard.png` | Vanguard — muscular aesthetic |
| `titan.png` | Titan — powerful, dense mass |
| `ascetic.png` | Ascetic — supple, mobile |

**Format:** transparent-background PNG (or WebP renamed to .png), portrait
orientation, at least 600×900 px. Full body, character roughly centered.

**Licensing — read this before adding files:** only use art you have the right
to ship commercially — art you commissioned, art you generated with an AI tool
under its commercial terms, or stock art with a proper license. Do NOT use
images of existing copyrighted characters (Goku, Sung Jin-Woo, etc.) taken from
the internet — likeness infringement gets apps removed from the Play Store and
exposes the account owner to legal claims, whether or not the character is
named in-app.
